# Treasurer (Society Admin) - Master Workflows & Responsibilities

## 1. The Boundary & End Goal
* **The Boundary:** The Treasurer operates strictly within the financial tables of their isolated PostgreSQL tenant schema (`schema_society_alpha`). They cannot edit resident access rights, change physical hardware configurations, or view the platform-level SaaS billing.
* **The End Goal:** To maintain 100% accurate accounts receivable (inflow) and accounts payable (outflow), ensure zero revenue leakage through automated defaulter tracking, and generate audit-ready financial statements for the Annual General Meeting (AGM).

## 2. Authentication & Access
* **Web Login:** Authenticates via the society's secure web portal (`/society_alpha/admin/login`).
* **Role Guard:** Granted the `role_id` of `TREASURER`. This role has exclusive write-access to the `maintenance_fees`, `expenses`, `invoices`, and `payment_gateways` tables.
* **Mobile App:** Primarily uses the web dashboard for heavy data entry, but can use the mobile app to quickly view daily collection stats or approve minor digital expense slips.

## 3. Accounts Receivable: Income & Collections (The Inflow)
*The Treasurer defines how much the society earns and ensures every rupee is collected.*
* **Fee Structure Configuration:** Sets the automated billing logic. Configures differential pricing based on flat type (e.g., ₹3/sqft for 2BHK, fixed ₹5000 for Shops).
* **Invoice Generation:** Triggers (or monitors the CRON job for) the monthly generation of maintenance fee invoices for all mapped `user_unit_mapping` accounts.
* **Payment Gateway Management:** Monitors the Razorpay (or equivalent) dashboard integration. Tracks successful UPI/Card/NetBanking digital payments and ensures the webhook instantly marks the resident's invoice as "Paid" and generates a digital PDF receipt.
* **Manual Receipt Entry:** If an older resident pays via physical Cheque or Cash, the Treasurer manually logs the transaction, updates the ledger, and triggers the digital receipt generation.
* **Fine & Penalty Issuance:** Issues ad-hoc fines based on reports from the Guard or Secretary (e.g., ₹500 fine for parking violation, ₹1000 fine for late-night noise). Fines are automatically appended to the resident's next billing cycle.
* **Amenity Revenue:** Tracks the income generated from paid amenity bookings (e.g., Community Hall deposits, Guest Parking fees).

## 4. Accounts Payable: Expenses & Payroll (The Outflow)
*The Treasurer ensures money leaves the society securely and legally.*
* **Vendor Bill Uploads:** Uploads physical or digital invoices from vendors (plumbers, lift AMC, electricity board) and categorizes them against the society's expense budget.
* **Payroll Processing:** Views the staff attendance logs generated by the Supervisor to calculate and process monthly salaries for security guards and housekeeping staff.
* **Maker-Checker Payouts (Security Rule):** For large payouts (e.g., above ₹50,000), the Treasurer acts as the "Maker". They prepare the Purchase Order (PO) and lock it. It cannot be paid out until the President (the "Checker") digitally signs it.
* **Petty Cash Management:** Logs small, daily physical cash expenses used by the Supervisor or Guards (e.g., buying cleaning supplies) to keep the petty cash ledger balanced.

## 5. Defaulter Management & Escalation
*The automated system handles reminders, but the Treasurer manages the legal escalation.*
* **Auto-Late Fees:** Configures the rule that automatically applies a late fee percentage (e.g., 2% per month) to invoices unpaid past the due date.
* **The Defaulter List:** Views a real-time, sortable dashboard of all flats with outstanding dues.
* **Service Disconnection Tracking:** If a flat reaches severe default (e.g., 90 days overdue), the Treasurer coordinates with the Secretary to issue a "Service Block" (suspending amenity access and vehicle fast-track entry).
* **Hardship Waivers:** Reviews resident requests for fee waivers (due to extreme circumstances) and submits them to the President for final approval before adjusting the ledger.

## 6. Financial Reporting & Reconciliation (The Ledger)
* **Bank Reconciliation:** Compares the society's physical bank statement with the software's digital ledger to ensure every Razorpay settlement matches the actual bank deposit.
* **Budget vs. Actuals:** Tracks the approved annual budget against real-time spending to alert the committee if they are overspending on categories like "Diesel for Generator" or "Lift Maintenance".
* **Tax & Audit Preparation:** Generates exportable, standardized reports (Income/Expense, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance) required by the society's Chartered Accountant (CA) for annual filing.

## 7. The AGM Financial Handover (The End Goal)
* **Publishing the Books:** Before the AGM, the Treasurer finalizes the year's P&L (Profit & Loss) statement and publishes it to the Notice Board for all residents to review.
* **Reserve Fund Planning:** Presents the status of the "Sinking Fund" or "Reserve Fund" (money saved for massive future repairs like repainting the building).
* **Democratic Handover:** If a new Treasurer is elected during the AGM, the outgoing Treasurer hands over the digital ledger cleanly, ensuring no pending reconciliations or orphaned vendor bills are left behind.


## 8. IoT Hardware Financial Triggers (Inputs & Outputs)
*The Treasurer does not manage the technical health of the hardware. Instead, they use the IoT infrastructure to automate consumption billing (smart meters) and enforce financial compliance (blocking access for defaulters).*

### A. Hardware Inputs (Data Flowing from Edge Pi -> Treasurer Ledger)
*This is the telemetry data translated directly into financial charges on a resident's invoice.*
* **Smart Utility Metering (Water/Electricity/Gas):** The Edge Pi ingests raw pulse data from smart meters attached to individual flats. The cloud converts these hardware pulses into Liters or kWh, which the Treasurer's system automatically multiplies by the tariff rate to generate the monthly utility bill.
* **Paid Amenity Usage Logs:** If a resident uses an RFID card to enter a "Pay-Per-Use" zone (e.g., the AC Community Hall or a Snooker Room), the Edge Pi sends the entry/exit timestamps to the cloud, which automatically calculates the hourly charge and posts it to the resident's ledger.
* **EV Charging Telemetry:** Data from society-owned electric vehicle charging stations. The hardware reports the kWh consumed by a specific resident's RFID tag, generating an automated charging fee.
* **Visitor Overstay / Paid Parking:** The ANPR (AI Camera) logs when a visitor's car enters and leaves. If the society charges for visitor parking after 4 hours, the hardware timestamps automatically trigger a parking fee appended to the host resident's monthly bill.
* **Hardware Replacement Billing:** When the Secretary issues a new physical RFID card to replace a lost one, the hardware registry automatically triggers a "New Card Issuance Fee" (e.g., ₹200) in the Treasurer's ledger for that specific flat.

### B. Hardware Outputs (Commands Flowing from Treasurer Ledger -> Edge Pi)
*These are the automated "Enforcement" commands the financial engine sends down to the physical hardware.*
* **The Defaulter Block (Service Suspension):** The most powerful tool the Treasurer has. If a resident's outstanding dues cross the configured threshold (e.g., 90 days overdue), the financial system automatically pushes a command to the Edge Pi to downgrade the resident's RFID and ANPR access. *(e.g., The boom barrier will no longer auto-open for their car, forcing them to manually check-in with the guard, and their access to the gym/pool is physically locked).*
* **Prepaid Utility Cutoff:** If the society uses a prepaid wallet system for electricity or piped gas, the moment the resident's digital wallet hits ₹0, the Treasurer's module fires an MQTT payload to the Edge Pi to trigger the physical relay, shutting off the flat's power/gas valve until a recharge is paid.
* **Paid Amenity Provisioning:** When a resident successfully pays for a venue booking (e.g., paying ₹5,000 for the Clubhouse on Saturday from 6 PM - 10 PM), the financial system sends a scheduled command to the Edge Pi. The local hardware will *only* unlock the clubhouse doors for that resident's specific RFID card during that exact paid time window.
