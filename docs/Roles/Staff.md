# Staff (Electrician / Cleaner / Plumber) - Master Workflows & Responsibilities

## 1. The Boundary & End Goal
* **The Boundary:** The Staff operates exclusively via the Mobile App. They cannot see society financial data, other residents' contact details, or administrative settings. They only see the tasks assigned specifically to them or their department.
* **The End Goal:** To complete assigned maintenance or cleaning tickets within the time limit, maintain their personal "Performance Rating" from residents, and ensure their attendance is logged for payroll.

## 2. Authentication & Shift Start
* **Mobile Login:** Authenticates using Phone Number + OTP. 
* **Attendance Clock-In:** Upon arriving at the society, the staff member must scan their RFID card at the Security Gate or scan a "Daily Attendance QR Code" in the Supervisor's office. This triggers the `SHIFT_START` timestamp in the database.
* **Status Toggle:** The staff member marks themselves as "Online" (Available for tasks) or "Busy" (Currently working).

## 3. Task Management (The Daily "To-Do" List)
*This is where the staff spends 90% of their time in the app.*
* **New Task Notification:** Receives a push notification when the Supervisor or a Resident raises a ticket (e.g., "Tap leakage in Flat 302").
* **Task Acceptance:** The staff member clicks "Accept" to acknowledge they are heading to the location. This updates the status for the Resident and Supervisor to "Staff en route."
* **Location Mapping:** The app shows the specific Wing, Floor, and Flat number where the work is required.

## 4. Execution & Proof of Work (The Resolution Loop)
*To prevent disputes, the system requires physical evidence.*
* **Before-Photo:** For certain tasks, the staff must take a "Before" photo (e.g., the broken pipe or the dirty hallway).
* **Work Timer:** The staff clicks "Start Work" to begin the resolution. The system tracks how long the repair takes to optimize future SLAs.
* **After-Photo:** Once the repair/cleaning is finished, the staff *must* upload a photo of the completed work.
* **Completion Signature:** In high-end societies, the staff can ask the resident to sign directly on the phone screen or provide a 4-digit "Job Completion OTP" generated by the resident's app.

## 5. Helpdesk & Resident Interaction
* **In-App Chat:** Can send pre-defined quick replies to the resident (e.g., "I am outside the door," "I need to buy a spare part"). 
* **Resident Rating:** After a job is closed, the staff member can see their average rating (1-5 stars) and feedback. High ratings are used by the Treasurer to calculate performance bonuses.

## 6. Inventory & Parts Request
* **Spare Parts Request:** If a repair requires a new part (like a 10W LED bulb or a PVC valve), the staff raises a request in the app.
* **Supervisor Approval:** The Supervisor receives the request and approves the "Issue from Stock." The staff member picks it up and the inventory is automatically deducted.

## 7. Personal Profile & Payroll Transparency
* **Attendance Calendar:** The staff can view their own attendance history—seeing exactly how many days they worked, how many leaves they took, and any "Late Entry" flags.
* **Payment Status:** Views a simplified summary of their upcoming salary based on the days worked, ensuring they have transparency before the Treasurer processes payroll.

## 8. Emergency Role
* **Siren Alerts:** If a "Fire" or "Global Emergency" is triggered by the President, the Staff App vibrates continuously. 
* **Evacuation Task:** The staff receives specific emergency instructions (e.g., "Cleaners to check Tower A staircases for elderly residents").


## 9. IoT Interaction & Task Verification (Inputs & Outputs)
*The Staff does not manage the hardware. Instead, they interact with physical IoT touchpoints across the society to prove their attendance, unlock utility areas, and verify that they have physically visited the site of a repair.*

### A. Hardware Inputs (Data Flowing from Edge Pi/Sensors -> Staff App)
*This is the automated telemetry that directly generates physical work for the staff.*
* **IoT-Triggered Maintenance Tasks:** Direct alerts generated by society hardware without human intervention. For example: 
    * A smart ultrasonic sensor inside a basement trash bin hits 90% capacity, automatically pushing an "Empty Basement 2 Bin" task to the Cleaner's app.
    * A water pressure sensor detects a drop, pushing a "Check Pump Room Valve" task to the Plumber.
* **Access Status Feedback:** Real-time feedback on their device if their physical access is denied. If an electrician tries to scan their RFID on the main server room door but lacks clearance, the app receives the rejection log (e.g., "Access Denied: Insufficient Privilege").
* **Emergency Hardware Triggers:** If the IoT fire sensors trigger the building's physical sirens, the Edge Pi simultaneously forces the Staff App into "Emergency Mode," overriding their current tasks with evacuation or physical assistance duties.

### B. Hardware Outputs (Physical Actions Flowing from Staff -> Edge Pi/Hardware)
*These are the physical interactions the staff must perform with the hardware to do their jobs and get paid.*
* **The "Clock-In" (Biometric/RFID Auth):** The foundational hardware interaction. Staff must press their thumb on a biometric scanner or tap their UHF RFID card at the physical gate reader to log their `SHIFT_START` in the database. Without this physical hardware handshake, they cannot accept tasks in the app.
* **NFC / QR Proof of Presence (Checkpoint Tapping):** To prove they actually did the work, staff must physically scan an NFC tag or QR code permanently affixed to the asset (e.g., scanning the tag stuck to the side of the 3rd-floor electrical panel). This sends a GPS-verified timestamp to the Edge Pi, proving the staff member was physically standing in front of the broken item.
* **Restricted Utility Access:** Using their designated Staff RFID card to trigger electrical relays on restricted doors. While residents can open lobby doors, the Staff RFID card is the only key configured in the Edge Pi to unlock "Service Elevators," "Pump Rooms," or "Rooftop Access Hatches."
* **Smart Inventory/Key Cabinets (Advanced):** If the society uses IoT key lockers, the staff scans their ID on the locker's hardware to release the physical keys for a specific resident's flat or a specific toolbox. The hardware logs exactly who took the physical item and when it was returned.
