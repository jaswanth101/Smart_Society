# Supervisor (Society Operations) - Master Workflows & Responsibilities

## 1. The Boundary & End Goal
* **The Boundary:** The Supervisor operates within the operational tables of the tenant schema (`schema_society_alpha`). They have full control over staff, tasks, and asset maintenance but have zero access to society bank accounts, legal member documents, or platform-level settings.
* **The End Goal:** To ensure the physical society runs like a well-oiled machine, ensuring 100% staff attendance, maintaining 100% uptime for critical hardware (Lifts, Pumps, Generators), and resolving every resident complaint within the established SLA.

## 2. Authentication & Access
* **Web & Mobile Login:** Authenticates via the society portal. This role is highly mobile; the Supervisor uses the Web Panel for planning and the Mobile App for on-ground verification.
* **Role Guard:** Granted the `role_id` of `SUPERVISOR`. This role has write-access to `tasks`, `attendance_logs`, `assets`, and `inventory` tables.

## 3. Staff & Workforce Management (The Daily Roster)
*The Supervisor is the direct manager of all on-site manpower (Guards, Cleaners, Electricians).*
* **Duty Roster Planning:** Creates and manages shift schedules for all staff members. Assigns specific guards to specific gates (e.g., Gate 1, Tower B Lobby).
* **Attendance Oversight:** Monitors real-time clock-in/out data generated by staff RFID/Biometric scans. Manually adjusts attendance in case of hardware failure or forgotten cards.
* **Leave Management:** Receives and approves/rejects digital leave applications from the staff via the Staff App.
* **Performance Tracking:** Views aggregated resident ratings for individual staff members to identify top performers or those needing training.

## 4. Task Management & Operations (The Work Engine)
*This is the core CRUD loop for the Supervisor.*
* **Task Creation & Assignment:** Breaks down the society's daily needs into digital tasks (e.g., "Clean Pool Area," "Check Generator Fuel"). Assigns these to specific staff members with a deadline.
* **Proof of Work Verification:** Reviews photos uploaded by staff upon task completion. Re-opens the task if the work is unsatisfactory.
* **Daily Rounds:** Uses the mobile app to scan QR codes at various checkpoints (Water Tanks, Electrical Room) to log that a physical inspection was performed.

## 5. Helpdesk & Complaint Resolution (The Fixer)
*The Supervisor owns the "Resolved" status of every resident issue.*
* **Ticket Triage:** Receives new resident complaints (Plumbing, Electrical, Seepage). Assigns the ticket to the relevant technician.
* **SLA Monitoring:** Tracks the countdown timer on every ticket. Receives push notifications when a ticket is 1 hour away from breaching the SLA (Service Level Agreement).
* **Resident Communication:** Updates residents via the app if a part needs to be ordered or if a technician is delayed.
* **Ticket Closure:** Verifies that the staff has uploaded a "Resolved" photo and confirms with the resident before officially closing the ticket in the system.

## 6. Asset & Inventory Management (The Custodian)
*Managing the physical equipment of the society.*
* **Asset Registry:** Maintains a digital log of all society assets (Lifts, Motors, Transformers, Gym Equipment) including their installation dates and warranty info.
* **Preventive Maintenance (AMC):** Schedules recurring maintenance tasks based on AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) dates. Triggers alerts when a lift or fire extinguisher service is due.
* **Consumables Inventory:** Tracks the stock of cleaning supplies, light bulbs, and diesel. Logs "Stock-Out" events when items are used for a task.
* **Refill Logs:** Manually logs diesel refills for the generator, which the Treasurer later reconciles against the bill.

## 7. Security & IoT Supervision
*The Supervisor supports the Guard and ensures the tech is working.*
* **Hardware Health Check:** Views a dashboard showing the "Online/Offline" status of all RFID readers, ANPR cameras, and the Edge Pi server. Reports malfunctions to the Super Admin.
* **Incident Review:** Reviews "Blacklisted" vehicle entry attempts or late-night security alerts flagged by the Guard or the AI system.
* **Visitor Audit:** Performs random audits of the Visitor Logs to ensure guards are taking clear photos and entering correct phone numbers.

## 8. Emergency Response
* **SOS Triage:** One of the primary recipients of the "SOS/Panic" alert. Coordinates the physical response of the guards to the location of the alert.
* **Utility Alerts:** Receives high-priority notifications from IoT sensors (e.g., "Water Tank Empty," "Gas Leak Detected") and immediately dispatches staff to resolve the physical cause.


## 9. IoT Asset Health & Operational Telemetry (Inputs & Outputs)
*The Supervisor acts as the chief mechanic and dispatcher. They interact with the physical hardware to monitor the health of peripheral devices (cameras, gates), track staff movement, and remotely control physical society assets.*

### A. Hardware Inputs (Data Flowing from Edge Pi -> Supervisor Dashboard)
*This is the operational telemetry the Supervisor uses to assign maintenance tasks and track staff.*
* **Peripheral Malfunction Alerts:** Unlike the Super Admin who monitors the main Edge Pi server, the Supervisor gets alerted when *peripherals* break. (e.g., "Gate 2 RFID Reader Unresponsive," "Tower C Lift Camera Video Loss," or "Boom Barrier Motor Overload"). These automatically generate high-priority maintenance tickets.
* **Staff Biometric / RFID Attendance:** Real-time data streams from the fingerprint/RFID scanners used by guards, cleaners, and electricians. This populates the live "Who is on Campus" dashboard and calculates shift hours.
* **Asset & Utility Triggers:** Direct readings from IoT-enabled society equipment. (e.g., "Underground Water Tank at 10%," "Diesel Generator (DG) Started Automatically," or "Fire Pump Pressure Drop"). 
* **NFC / QR Guard Tour Logs:** The timestamped and GPS-verified pings generated when the Security Guard physically taps their tablet to NFC tags placed at critical checkpoints (like the basement corners or electrical rooms) during their night patrol.
* **Smart Bin / Waste Telemetry (If applicable):** Alerts from ultrasonic sensors inside large society waste bins indicating they are 90% full, triggering a task for the housekeeping staff to empty them.

### B. Hardware Outputs (Commands Flowing from Supervisor -> Edge Pi)
*These are the physical overrides the Supervisor uses to perform maintenance, test systems, and manage daily operations.*
* **Maintenance / Service Mode Override:** The ability to put a specific piece of hardware into "Maintenance Mode." (e.g., Sending a command to lock the Boom Barrier in the 'UP' position for 2 hours while a mechanic replaces the motor, bypassing all normal access rules).
* **Manual IoT Relay Controls:** Bypassing automated schedules to manually trigger physical assets via the Edge Pi. (e.g., Tapping a button on the app to turn on the Tennis Court floodlights, start the backup water pump, or turn off the landscape sprinklers during a rainstorm).
* **Hardware Diagnostic / Siren Testing:** Sending a localized test command to physical hardware to verify repairs. (e.g., Triggering a 3-second test blast of the fire siren in Tower A, or forcing an RFID reader to reboot after a wire replacement).
* **Temporary Contractor Provisioning:** Pushing a strictly time-bound (e.g., "Valid for 4 Hours") digital access token to the hardware for a third-party vendor, like a lift mechanic or pest control team, so they can access restricted service areas without needing the Guard to escort them.
